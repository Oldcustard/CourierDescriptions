# Courier Descriptions v1.0.0

**The courier tells you who gave him your mail.**

## Latest version
https://github.com/Oldcustard/CourierDescriptions/releases/tag/v1.0.0

## Features

**Generated sender lines** — When the courier delivers something he has no "who sent it" voiceline for, he remarks on whoever handed it to him. Written at delivery time, so it fits the letter that actually arrived — including ones other mods generate.

> *"Redguard barmaid from the Mare gave me this, sounding desperate."*
> *"Jarl Balgruuf handed me this himself, looking like he had something heavy on his mind."*

**His own voice** — Real dialogue, not narration: correct voice type, lip sync, subtitles, and the vanilla lines still play.

**He never reads your mail** — The letter's text only tells the mod what sort of person sent it. He describes them, never the contents.

**Knows who's who** — When a letter names its sender, they're resolved to the real NPC, whose bio informs the description. How familiar he sounds scales with their standing — a Jarl he recognises, an innkeeper he can place, a farmer stays a stranger.

**Invents nothing** — With no sender identified he takes his detail from the handover, not from a guess at a face the game may later contradict.

## Installation

Install with a mod manager. ESL-flagged — costs no load order slot. Safe to add mid-playthrough.

**SkyrimNet's "Enable TTS For Silent NPCs" must be on** (WebUI → Settings → Voice) — it's what gives the courier's unvoiced line a voice. With it off he says nothing.

## Known issue

The line sits in a fixed 13-second dialogue slot, so expect a pause before it while speech generates, and quiet after. Barely noticeable on fast TTS; a slow cloud provider may overlap his closing line.
